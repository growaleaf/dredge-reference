Sources & caveats:
  • Fish and aberration tables (67 base fish across The Marrows, Gale Cliffs, Stellar Basin, Twisted Strand, Devil's Spine, Open Ocean and the Exotic category, plus 61 aberrations) -- Gameplay Tips, DREDGE Encyclopedia Guide, published 2023-04-04, fetched 2026-11-16.
This source predates DREDGE's paid expansions. DLC fish from the Pale Reach and Iron Rig content are not covered on this page -- no second reachable source with real, published values for those fish was found. dredge.wiki.gg and dredge.fandom.com both returned 403 Forbidden when fetched directly.
Sell price shown is the source's AVERAGE/base rate for that fish. The source itself states the actual sale value varies with the size (quality) of the individual catch -- treat every price here as a baseline, not a guarantee of what you'll get at the merchant.
A handful of rows in the source had internal typos (a duplicated or truncated base-fish name). Those rows carry a note explaining exactly what the source said and how it was resolved; nothing was invented to fill a gap -- any value the source does not publish is shown as "not published".
